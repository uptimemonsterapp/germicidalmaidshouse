Our Eco-Friendly Cleaning Service Checklist

No vague promises — here is exactly what our team covers during your eco-friendly cleaning appointment.

Bedroom

  • Dust all surfaces, furniture & décor
  • Wipe mirrors, glass & light switches
  • Vacuum & mop floors
  • Make beds / change linens on request
  • Empty trash & tidy

Bathroom

  • Scrub & disinfect toilets, tubs & showers
  • Clean sinks, counters & fixtures
  • Polish mirrors & glass
  • Sanitize high-touch points
  • Mop & deodorize floors
  • Plant-based disinfectants

Kitchen

  • Clean & sanitize countertops & backsplash
  • Wipe appliance exteriors
  • Clean sink, faucet & inside microwave
  • Wipe cabinet fronts & handles
  • Sweep & mop floors
  • Food-safe surface products

Living Room

  • Dust furniture, shelves & electronics
  • Vacuum carpets & upholstery
  • Mop hard floors
  • Clean windowsills & blinds
  • Fluff pillows & tidy spaces
  • HEPA-filter vacuuming
