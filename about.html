About Germicidal Maids House Cleaning

Our Story

Built on Trust, Grown by Happy Homes

Germicidal Maids House Cleaning was founded on a simple frustration: too many Orange County families had been let down by no-shows, inconsistent quality and last-minute cancellations from cleaning services. We set out to change everything about that experience.

Today, our professional, background-checked teams serve homes across Orange County from our Laguna Hills office — always arriving on time, following transparent room-by-room checklists, and standing behind every visit with a 100% satisfaction guarantee. Residential cleaning is our specialty, whether that's a weekly recurring service, a detailed deep cleaning, or helping hosts shine with Airbnb turnover cleaning.
