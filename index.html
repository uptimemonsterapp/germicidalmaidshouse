Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

No. Many clients provide entry instructions and go about their day. Our insured, background-checked team treats your home with total respect whether you are home or away.

Kitchens, bathrooms, dusting all surfaces, vacuuming and mopping floors, mirrors, and tidying every room. Add-ons like inside the oven, fridge or windows are available on any visit.

Yes. We offer green, non-toxic products through our eco-friendly cleaning service, and every standard product we use is family- and pet-safe.
