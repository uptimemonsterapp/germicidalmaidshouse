Our Deep Cleaning Service Checklist

No vague promises — here is exactly what our team covers during your deep cleaning appointment.

Bedroom

  • Dust all surfaces, furniture & décor
  • Wipe mirrors, glass & light switches
  • Vacuum & mop floors
  • Make beds / change linens on request
  • Empty trash & tidy
  • Dust vents, fans & baseboards
  • Clean under furniture

Bathroom

  • Scrub & disinfect toilets, tubs & showers
  • Clean sinks, counters & fixtures
  • Polish mirrors & glass
  • Sanitize high-touch points
  • Mop & deodorize floors
  • Scrub grout & tile
  • Remove soap scum & hard-water buildup

Kitchen

  • Clean & sanitize countertops & backsplash
  • Wipe appliance exteriors
  • Clean sink, faucet & inside microwave
  • Wipe cabinet fronts & handles
  • Sweep & mop floors
  • Degrease stovetop & hood
  • Clean inside microwave & appliance detailing

Dining & Living

  • Dust furniture, shelves & electronics
  • Vacuum carpets & upholstery
  • Mop hard floors
  • Clean windowsills & blinds
  • Fluff pillows & tidy spaces
  • Hand-wipe baseboards & door frames

Home Office & Playrooms

  • Dust desks, shelves & cords
  • Sanitize high-touch surfaces
  • Organize & tidy play areas
  • Vacuum under cushions

Add-ons

  • Inside oven & refrigerator
  • Interior windows & tracks
  • Wall spot-cleaning
  • Cabinet & drawer interiors
  • Garage sweep-out

FAQ

Deep Cleaning — Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a deep clean take?

Most homes take 4–8 labor hours depending on size and condition. We can send two cleaners to finish faster.

How often should I schedule a deep clean?

We recommend a deep clean every 3–6 months, or as the first visit before starting recurring service.

What's the difference between deep and standard cleaning?

Deep cleaning adds baseboards, vents, grout scrubbing, appliance detailing and hand-washing of reachable surfaces that standard visits maintain.
