Our Move Out Cleaning Service Checklist

No vague promises — here is exactly what our team covers during your move out cleaning appointment.

Kitchen

  • Clean & sanitize countertops & backsplash
  • Wipe appliance exteriors
  • Clean sink, faucet & inside microwave
  • Wipe cabinet fronts & handles
  • Sweep & mop floors
  • Inside oven & refrigerator
  • Inside cabinets & drawers

Bathrooms

  • Scrub & disinfect toilets, tubs & showers
  • Clean sinks, counters & fixtures
  • Polish mirrors & glass
  • Sanitize high-touch points
  • Mop & deodorize floors
  • Scrub grout & remove hard-water stains

Bedrooms & Closets

  • Dust all surfaces, furniture & décor
  • Wipe mirrors, glass & light switches
  • Vacuum & mop floors
  • Make beds / change linens on request
  • Empty trash & tidy
  • Wipe closet shelves & rods

Throughout the Home

  • Baseboards, doors & door frames
  • Light switches & outlet covers
  • Interior windows & sills
  • Vents, fans & light fixtures
  • Wall spot-cleaning

Floors

  • Vacuum all carpets & edges
  • Mop all hard floors
  • Detail corners & under appliances
